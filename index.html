One word makes the ad

Take a pair of wool socks. The product page says soft. A buyer’s review says his feet finally stay dry in winter. Soft describes the sock. Dry is why someone with cold feet buys it, so dry goes in the headline.

Questions

Do the ads work?

We can’t promise that before an ad has run. Only a test in your own ad account shows what sells. That’s why the first ones are free, and why after that you only pay for an ad that beats your best one.

What do you need from me?

Only your store’s address. We take the rest from your site, your reviews and the ads you already run.

Who makes them?

Loopworks is a small studio in the Netherlands, run by Efe Gevher. We research, write and design every ad, use AI tools where they help, and check each one before you see it.

Do you use AI images?

Yes, often for the whole picture, your product included. We draw it from your own product photos, check your label letter by letter, and nothing runs without your OK.

Will you quote my customers?

Only with your permission. You confirm that a review can be used before any ad with it runs.

Which sizes do I get?

4:5 (1080 × 1350) for the feed and 9:16 (1080 × 1920) for stories and reels.

Do you run the ads for me?

No. You run them in your own Meta ads account, so the budget and the results stay yours.

What happens after the free ones?

You only pay for an ad that beats your best one in a Meta A/B test in your own ad account. If it doesn’t win, you pay us nothing. The test runs on your normal ad budget.
