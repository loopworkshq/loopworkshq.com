Who we are

Loopworks is the trading name of Ömer Efe Gevher, a sole proprietorship registered in the Netherlands (KvK 42199416, VAT ID NL005576924B19), Oder 20 Box C9920, 2491 DC The Hague, Netherlands. Questions about your data: hello@loopworkshq.com.

This website

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other sites. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log a visitor's IP address for security purposes; we don't receive or use those logs.

What we collect, and why

  • Public information about your store: your website, product photos, customer reviews and the ads you run publicly (for example in Meta's Ad Library). We use it to make a sample ad for you.
  • Your business contact details: the email address your store publishes, and your name if you write to us. We use them to send you the sample ad and to talk about it.
  • Our emails with you, including a "no", so we never write to you again after you ask us to stop.
  • If you test an ad with us: the test result (a screenshot or a view-only look at your ad account) and the amount spent on our ad, to see whether it won and to work out the fee.
  • If you pay us: your business name, billing address and email, for the invoice. Card payments are handled by Stripe; we never see your full card number.

We use this information because it is needed to offer and deliver our service to businesses (our legitimate interest), to carry out our agreement with you once you say "go", and to keep the records Dutch law requires.

Who helps us

We share only what each one needs to do its job:

  • Zoho (email, EU data centre).
  • AI image tools, through Higgsfield, which receive your product photos to draw the ad scene.
  • Anthropic (Claude), an AI assistant that helps us research, write and check our work.
  • Stripe (invoices and payments).
  • GitHub (hosting this website).

Some of these companies process data outside the European Union, under their own safeguards for international transfers. We don't sell your data, and we don't use it for anyone else's advertising.

Test results

Your results stay between us. We keep a record of which kinds of ads win, without store names, so we learn what works.

How long we keep it

  • Outreach to a store that never replies: up to 12 months, then deleted.
  • A "no" or "stop": kept for as long as we send emails, only so we never contact you again.
  • Invoices and payment records: 7 years, as Dutch tax law requires.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to us using it, by writing to hello@loopworkshq.com. We'll answer within a month. You can also complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.
