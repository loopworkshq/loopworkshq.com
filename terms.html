When these terms apply

They apply once you reply "go" to our test email. That reply is your agreement; there is nothing to sign and no retainer.

The test

  • You run one of our ads against your best current ad in Meta's A/B test, in your own ad account, on your normal ad budget.
  • Meta's result decides. The ad with the lower cost per purchase wins. If your budget is small, we agree before the start to judge by cost per add-to-cart instead.
  • You send us a screenshot of the result, or give us view-only access so we can read it.

What you pay

  • If your ad wins: nothing.
  • If our ad wins: 10% of what you spend on it, for its first 3 months. The 3 months start when the test ends with our ad ahead.
  • If you choose to run one of our ads without a test, it counts as a win: the same 10% of what you spend on it, for its first 3 months.
  • No spend, no fee. If you pause the ad, you owe nothing for that time. After 3 months the ad is yours to keep running, free.
  • We invoice monthly, in US dollars, based on the spend Meta's Ads Manager shows for our ad. Payment by card within 14 days. No Dutch VAT is charged on services to businesses outside the Netherlands; the invoice says why.

What we can and can't promise

Most new ads don't beat a store's best one, and no one can promise that an ad will sell before it has run. That's why you pay only for an ad that wins, or one you choose to run without a test.

Our ad pictures are made with AI tools from your own product photos. We check every label letter by letter against your photos and use no AI-made people. Headlines may use a customer's words from your reviews; you decide whether to run them.

Your part

You decide which ads to run and how much to spend, and you run them in your own ad account under Meta's advertising rules. Tell us if anything in an ad is wrong for your store before you run it, and we'll fix it.
